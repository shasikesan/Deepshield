## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, editorial-grade verification environment engineered for deepfake detection, synthetic identity auditing, and media provenance analysis. Rejecting tired cybersecurity clichés—specifically the omnipresent fluorescent blues, cyan wireframes, and digital matrix tropes—this system treats synthetic media verification not as an IT network problem, but as an authoritative editorial and forensic standard.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with restrained **Glassmorphism** and high-end technical luxury:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep warm obsidian surfaces, crystalline typography, surgical negative space, and amber-gold luminescence.
- **Tone:** Incontrovertible, discreet, forensic, and premium. The interface communicates unassailable institutional trust, akin to an archival inspection instrument or a high-finance intelligence terminal.
- **Emotional Response:** Absolute certainty, cognitive calm, and focused precision amidst an informational landscape of synthetic deception.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in warm graphite obsidian foundations, strictly avoiding cold or standard cyber-blue hues.

### Foundational Surfaces & Text
- **Canvas Base (`#121214`):** Ultra-deep warm graphite obsidian providing absolute contrast without the sterile harshness of pitch black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#18181b`):** Primary card background, navigation bars, and structural panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#222226`):** Hover states, nested inspection frames, modal sheets, and floating toolbars.
- **Border Titanium Hairline (`#27272a`):** Base structural separator; low-contrast boundary definition.
- **Border Titanium Active (`#3f3f46`):** Focused states, active tabs, and elevated container edges.
- **Typography Primary (`#fafafa`):** High-readability pure off-white for headlines, active metrics, and primary labels.
- **Typography Muted (`#a1a1aa`):** Technical secondary metadata, timestamps, and confidence score captions.

### Diagnostic & Accent Palette
- **Primary Accent (`#f59e0b` / Warm Amber Gold):** Used for interactive triggers, primary focus states, cryptographic hash accents, and high-tier forensic controls.
- **Secondary Accent (`#10b981` / Emerald Provenance):** Reserved strictly for authentic content confirmations, cryptographically signed watermarks, verified biometric telemetry, and passing scores.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#f43f5e` / Sunset Vermilion):** Reserved strictly for synthetic media alerts, manipulated frame flags, adversarial artifacts, and high-risk notifications.

## Typography

The typography strategy pairs high-character geometric authority with razor-sharp editorial legibility:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a sculptural, modern technical presence without falling into brutalist clumsiness. Tight negative tracking gives hero verification statistics and panel titles intentionality and presence.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (`Geist`):** Provides clinical, ultra-refined neutral sans-serif readability optimized for dense forensic readouts, model explanation trees, and deep inspection logs.
- **Diagnostic Labels, Telemetry & Code (`JetBrains Mono`):** Handles all cryptographic checksums, timestamp logs, frame counters, percentage metrics, and micro-badges. Uppercase presentation with deliberate positive letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) anchors visual hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column fluid grid bounded by strict maximum container widths (1440px for forensic dashboards, 1200px for editorial reading panes):
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer canvas margins. Multi-pane inspect-and-verify layouts divide into asymmetric modular splits (e.g., 7 columns for real-time video spectrum rendering, 5 columns for forensic provenance telemetry).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Inspection viewports pin to top-level viewports; technical scorecards wrap into 4-column balanced card pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer margins. Dynamic floating panels collapse into drawer sheets; frame-by-frame timelines switch from horizontal scrubber ribbons to vertical stacked micro-event lists.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of **subtle tonal layering, frosted glass refraction, and hairline titanium bounds**:

- **Ground Plane (Level 0):** Pure `#121214` obsidian. Non-interactive, deeply recessed.
- **Card Tier 1 (Level 1):** Solid `#18181b` or translucent `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.75)` with `12px` backdrop blur and a `1px` continuous border of `#27272a`.
- **Card Tier 2 / Active Inspection (Level 2):** Elevated interactive modals or inspection overlays at `rgba(34, 34, 38, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop blur, a hairline border of `#3f3f46`, and an ambient diffused amber shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.05)`.
- **Alert & Detection States:** High-severity anomalies omit diffuse dark drop-shadows and instead leverage a subtle razor rim: `0 0 0 1px #f43f5e, 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored **Soft** shape aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`):
- Base interactive controls, cards, and input panels employ subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radii to maintain architectural tension and professional discipline.
- Pill forms (`9999px` radius) are strictly reserved for micro-badges, status verification chips, provenance labels, and floating scrubber heads to create contrast against structural rectilinear card modules.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Amber Button:** `#f59e0b` solid background, `#121214` high-contrast typography, `0.25rem` radius, subtle transition to `#d97706` on hover. Used for explicit user actions (e.g., "Analyze Artifact", "Sign Certificate").
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Translucent `#18181b` background with `1px` border `#3f3f46` and `#fafafa` text. Transitions to border `#f59e0b` on focus.
- **Alert Trigger:** Border `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)` on `#18181b`, with text in `#f43f5e`.

### Micro-Badges & Status Chips
- **Authentic Verification:** Pill-shaped badge with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` background, `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`, and `#10b981` text styled with `label-sm` in `JetBrains Mono`. Features a glowing 4px emerald indicator dot.
- **Synthetic Alert:** Pill-shaped badge with `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.1)` background, `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`, and `#f43f5e` text. Indicator pulses on detection events.
- **Analysis In-Progress:** Pill-shaped badge with `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)` background, `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`, and `#f59e0b` text.

### Cards & Forensic Glass Containers
- Frosted cards rendered in `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a `1px` hairline boundary in `#27272a`.
- Headers within containers leverage `headline-md` paired with a discrete metadata block rendered in `label-sm`.

### Inputs & Verification Upload Fields
- Field surfaces use `#121214` inset with `1px` border `#27272a`, transitioning to `#f59e0b` on focus with zero default browser outline.
- Placeholder text utilizes `#71717a` in `Geist`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square-profile check indicators (`0.125rem` radius) and circular radio rings crafted with `#27272a` borders. Upon activation, they fill with warm amber `#f59e0b` and an obsidian `#121214` inner glyph.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Frame-by-Frame Timeline Scrubber:** Horizontal obsidian strip `#18181b` demarcated with titanium hash marks `#27272a`. Manipulated audio/video segments are highlighted with vermilion segments (`#f43f5e`), while authentic segments retain hairline emerald markers (`#10b981`).
- **Confidence Metric Gauge:** Dual-concentric ring meter built with `#222226` background tracks and active stroke data paths colored in either `#10b981` (Authentic Confidence) or `#f43f5e` (Synthetic Manipulation Likelihood), accompanied by centered metrics in `Space Grotesk`.
